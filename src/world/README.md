# Procedural world

**Status (2026-10-22):** Phase 4 built scope was delivered on 2026-10-15 and the owner reports Phase 5 passed. Phase 9 Aohara work and Phase 6 vehicle visuals are authorized. Aohara has a 5×5, 640 m road grid, 16 blocks, 32 instanced high-rises, 22 authored lower-rise buildings and a stadium, plus tier-capped infill (C/B/A: 6/9/12 buildings). Traffic and pedestrians remain within existing caps (6/12/18 vehicles; 12/24/36 pedestrians): the five instanced vehicle silhouettes are passenger cars, city buses, rigid trucks, semi-trailer rigs, and farm tractors. Vehicles follow deterministic avenue loops and obey the central signal with class-length-aware queue offsets; pedestrians remain on central-block sidewalks. Actors have no moving collision, crossing right-of-way, or full AI recovery. Graph-aligned streetlamp spacing is 40 m (240 instanced poles), with 48 unbadged lamp-post banners and 18 abstract billboards. C/B/A local point-light caps are 6/8/12 total, partitioned between nearest streetlights and architectural sources; local lights are unshadowed. Aohara asphalt retains its bounded static wet-look clearcoat. The default start is Aohara Central Avenue; explicit rural Hinode starts and `?spawn=random` remain available. Seeded terrain, chunked LOD, rural districts and Yūhi Town remain intact. City lighting is not a weather, standing-water, traction or SSR system. Automated composition/budget checks do not establish manual driveability, owner-device visuals or device performance. Full traffic AI, pedestrian crossings/right-of-way, grade-separated links and highways remain future work.

## Responsibility

Seeded terrain and roads, chunk identity, worker jobs, streaming, LOD, collider coverage, floating
origin — and the pluggable definition of what a scene is made of.

## Implemented

| File                        | Responsibility                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `WorldContent.ts`           | `SceneContent`, the seam between the environment and scene content, with optional daylight, frame-update, streaming, statistics and collision hooks                                                                                                                     |
| `terrain-height.ts`         | The one height function: integer-hash deterministic fBm hills + the Hinode-dake cone + the exactly-flat spawn plateau, plus the height/slope splat colours. Pure; everything samples it                                                                                 |
| `terrain-chunks.ts`         | The pure streaming planner: chunk coordinates, LOD-by-ring, the visual plan and the physics window. No three.js, no Rapier, so the plan is unit-testable                                                                                                                |
| `terrain-geometry.ts`       | Chunk mesh building: halo-sampled grid normals (bit-identical shared borders), vertex-colour splat, skirts against LOD cracks                                                                                                                                           |
| `TerrainWorld.ts`           | The stateful streamer: budgeted builds (≤2/frame), in-place LOD swaps, eviction, two splat materials (near ring raymarches the scanned displacement — POM), diagnostics stats                                                                                           |
| `terrain-generation.ts`     | The worker protocol: rebuild-the-same-sampler init, request/result messages, transferable buffer handling — deterministic across threads by construction                                                                                                                |
| `terrain-worker.ts`         | Thin worker entry: owns a generation sampler and answers requests; all logic lives in the testable protocol module                                                                                                                                                      |
| `chunk-generator.ts`        | Main-thread worker pool (2 workers, staleness rejection, watchdog, permanent sync fallback on any failure)                                                                                                                                                              |
| `origin.ts`                 | The floating origin: decides when the local physics/render space slides (whole 128 m chunks past 2048 m) under the eternal world coordinates                                                                                                                            |
| `districts.ts`              | District resolver: which §5 place (if any) influences a world point, as per-species density multipliers with soft edges                                                                                                                                                 |
| `vegetation.ts`             | Deterministic instanced scatter: hash-decided placement per cell, sampler-gated (road/treeline/slope), species registry + streaming window                                                                                                                              |
| `vegetation-placement.ts`   | The pure placement function (renderer-free): same chunk in, same forest out — shared by the renderer and the collision field                                                                                                                                            |
| `tree-collision.ts`         | Destructive-vegetation truth: trunk-vs-chassis collision, hit-point damage (bend → snap → shatter), rock-solid response impulses                                                                                                                                        |
| `town.ts`                   | Yūhi Town: deterministic building plots (road-clear, slope-gated, tree-free) and roadside poles from the solved network; buildings solid, poles fold on accumulated hits (0.3 s cooldown)                                                                               |
| `DrivingWorld.ts`           | Driving content: streamed terrain/vegetation, rural town and roads, plus Aohara's 5×5 grid, sidewalks, crossings and night block; forwards daylight, updates, collisions, stats and disposal                                                                            |
| `road-network.ts`           | The pure road graph builder: classed/wide per-edge profiles, Catmull-Rom smoothing, pinned junction elevations, grade clamping, spatial index, and road-surface sampling                                                                                                |
| `world-terrain.ts`          | The composed sampler the game uses: base terrain with the solved road profiles carved into the height function, so meshes, colliders, and grip share one ground truth                                                                                                   |
| `road-geometry.ts`          | Ribbon geometry (pavement, shoulders, edge lines, raised urban sidewalks) and centre-dash generation from solved profiles                                                                                                                                               |
| `RoadWorld.ts`              | The visual road network: per-class widths, batched road/sidewalk ribbons, instanced urban crossings, junction discs, async CC0 surfaces, Aohara wet-look clearcoat and road stats                                                                                       |
| `RouteWorld.ts`             | The Phase 3 validation route (fixed vista for the SSIM baseline)                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `urban-night.ts`            | Phase 9 Aohara kit: nearest-focus street/architectural pointlights, graph-aligned lamps, banners/billboards, tier-capped building infill/windows/facade detail, stadium, local Gaussian diffraction and signalized junction; mounts in look-dev and driving-world paths |
| `urban-activity.ts`         | Bounded Phase 9 actors: five tier-capped instanced vehicle classes follow signal-aware loops; optional shared Rapier bodies provide player/traffic and traffic/traffic response plus brief impact animation; pedestrians stay on sidewalks, with no crossing AI         |
| `traffic-vehicle-models.ts` | Code-authored, merged Three.js geometry for unbadged passenger cars, city buses, rigid trucks, semi-trailer rigs, and farm tractors; one instanced mesh per class with three material groups                                                                            |

The seam exists because the project needs more than one answer to "what is the scene made of?" —
the studio look-dev grid in `src/lab/` wants identical lighting and post-processing over entirely
different content. Phase 4's terrain arrived behind the same interface, as drawn.

## Planned work

Phase 9 remains open: review the lighting, infill and activity increment in a browser and on an owner device; validate manual Aohara driving; add traffic recovery refinements and pedestrian crossing/right-of-way only after explicit design and clearance checks; continue bounded district expansion; add expressways, tunnels, bridges/flyovers only after grade, clearance and collision validation. City traffic now uses the shared Rapier world, with bounded dynamic bodies and collision feedback; pedestrians remain tier-capped sidewalk actors. Aohara uses the normal streamed terrain/physics sampler and prepare-ahead window, but driveability, owner-device visuals and performance are not yet validated. Weather/seasons and unrelated final polish remain separately gated.
