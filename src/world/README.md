# Procedural world

**Status (2026-10-23):** Phase 4 terrain/roads are delivered and the owner reports Phase 5 passed. Aohara and its 5×5, 640 m grid, 16 blocks, 32 high-rises, 22 lower-rise shells, stadium, tier-capped infill, street furniture and localized night-light budget remain intact; Central Avenue is the default start. The five instanced traffic classes use standard tier budgets of 6/12/18 vehicles at C/B/A; the authorized menu setting selects Off, Light, Standard or Busy (up to 1.5× standard) without changing pedestrian budgets. Deterministic signal-aware routes and bounded Rapier contacts still produce physical response plus transient animation, never mesh deformation. Pedestrians remain sidewalk actors without crossing right-of-way AI. The optional Hinode Touge route and existing rural districts/Yūhi Town remain. The owner has authorized only the requested Phase 8 pause/home menu and named options; Phase 7 weather and unrelated Phase 8 polish remain gated. Automated checks do not establish visual quality, manual driveability or device performance.

## Responsibility

Seeded terrain and roads, chunk identity, worker jobs, streaming, LOD, collider coverage, floating
origin — and the pluggable definition of what a scene is made of.

The seam exists because the project needs more than one answer to "what is the scene made of?" —
the studio look-dev grid in `src/lab/` wants identical lighting and post-processing over entirely
different content. Phase 4's terrain arrived behind the same interface, as drawn.

## Planned work

Phase 9 remains open: review the lighting, infill and activity increment in a browser and on an owner device; validate manual Aohara driving; refine bounded post-collision traffic recovery and pedestrian crossing/right-of-way only after explicit signal and clearance checks; continue bounded district expansion; add expressways, tunnels, bridges/flyovers only after grade, clearance and collision validation. City traffic now uses the shared Rapier world, signal-aware loops and mixed-class following gaps, with bounded dynamic bodies and collision feedback; pedestrians remain tier-capped sidewalk actors. Aohara uses the normal streamed terrain/physics sampler and prepare-ahead window, but driveability, owner-device visuals and performance are not yet validated. Weather/seasons and unrelated final polish remain separately gated.
