# Credits and attributions

This file lists every third-party asset in Endless Driver, its creator, its licence, and — where a licence requires
it — the credit line that must appear in the shipped game's credits screen.

**Licence policy:** CC0 is preferred. Free licences that clearly permit intended use and redistribution—including CC BY 4.0 with required attribution, including for an owner-approved hero vehicle—are allowed. See [`docs/ASSET_POLICY.md`](docs/ASSET_POLICY.md) and [`docs/ASSET_SOURCES.md`](docs/ASSET_SOURCES.md).

**Project-generated assets** are not third-party content: the rendering look LUT is generated by this repository's
own script, from a formula written for this project, and is marked `Project-Generated` in the manifest. It is listed
below for completeness rather than to satisfy a licence.

---

## Required credit lines

When a CC BY asset is present, the manifest-derived **Assets used** section below contains its exact required attribution. That text is also displayed in the in-game credits view and must accompany redistribution.
